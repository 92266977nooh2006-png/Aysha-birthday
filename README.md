# Aysha's Birthday Website 💗

This version includes all 9 photos you uploaded, the Darkhaast MP3, the floating music player inspired by your reference screenshot, a scrapbook (with the middle “You + me” card removed), a moving starry backdrop on the second slide, a 17 → 18 birthday reveal, animations, and the 12 October birthday countdown.

## Music behaviour
- The website tries to start Darkhaast when it loads.
- Browsers may block autoplay with sound. If that happens, tap once anywhere on the page or use the round play button.
- The player includes play/pause, current time, duration, and a seek bar.
- The track is included at `assets/darkhaast.mp3`.

## Photos
All eight uploaded photos are already added to the gallery and four also appear in the scrapbook. Their files are in the `assets` folder.

## Free hosting
Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and the complete `assets` folder to the root of your GitHub repository, then enable GitHub Pages in Settings → Pages. The included song is about 15 MB, so it may take a little time to load over mobile data.

## Silent background animation
- The supplied video is included as `assets/background-animation.mp4`.
- It loops automatically in the background with its audio muted.
- The memories gallery now contains nine photos, arranged evenly in three columns on mobile and desktop.
